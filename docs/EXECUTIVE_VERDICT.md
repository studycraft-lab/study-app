# Executive verdict

## Decision

**Take the 16-week sabbatical only as a gated validation sprint. Pursue with a narrower wedge: ICSE Grades 5-7, Science and Social Studies, parent-initiated, typed answers, one chapter at a time, with adult review for the first 25 families.**

**3 September evidence update:** Four founder-household chapters have been tuned successfully enough to justify a ten-chapter technical POC across Grades 3 and 6. A founder-run Gemini comparison was slow, objective-heavy, weak on genuine subjective input, easy to coax into revealing answers, and poor as a parent-child workflow. This lowers technical and generic-AI substitution risk. It does not establish external demand.

Do not spend the sabbatical building an all-board Grades 3-10 AI tutor, handwriting/voice/stylus input, a publisher corpus, or a school sales motion. Grade 3 should be a usability test, not target-market proof.

## Why this is worth testing

- The repository already has the meaningful core loop: validated question banks, nine question types, objective and rubric-based subjective marking, mastery/coverage, review scheduling, citations, parent controls, and bad-question reporting.
- Indian households demonstrably spend on supplementary education. The government's 2025 education survey found 27% of students used private coaching and reported average urban coaching expenditure of INR 3,988 annually ([official PIB summary](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2160863&lang=2&reg=3)). This proves a spending category, not demand for this product.
- The proposed wedge addresses a concrete job: help a child practise written answers from the exact chapter while reducing the parent's question-making and checking time.
- ICSE is accessible to the founder and less standardized than CBSE, making exact-edition handling potentially valuable.

## Why this is not yet a business

- Near-free tools already turn uploaded sources into study material. [NotebookLM](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16164461?hl=en) is source-grounded, and [ChatGPT Study Mode](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11780217-using-study-mode-in-chatgpt) can work from uploaded notes and textbook excerpts. “AI-generated questions” is not a moat.
- The real competitors are parent effort, worksheets/guidebooks, tuition, and low-cost board apps. Printed books can cost only a few hundred rupees; myCBSEguide currently advertises a Class 10 subscription at INR 699 ([myCBSEguide](https://mycbseguide.com/blog/online-course-for-class-10/)).
- Willingness to pay, repeat chapter use, child self-direction, upload friction, grading trust, and review/support cost are unproven.
- Private parent upload and family isolation reduce copyright exposure but do not automatically create a commercial licence. Child consent, data isolation, processor controls, retention, and incident handling need a counsel-reviewed beta posture.
- The original INR 2,499 annual model was an economic stress anchor, not evidence of willingness to pay. If the POC supports automated verification and risk-based review, substantially lower chapter-pack pricing becomes plausible; support and CAC remain decisive.

## The offer to validate

> “Send the exact chapter. Within 24 hours, receive a reviewed written-exam practice bank with source-linked questions and rubric-point feedback.”

- One free reviewed chapter, normally up to ten pages.
- Then **INR 99 for the next three chapters/30 pages**, one child, valid 30 days.
- Test a second real purchase at **INR 199 for five chapters** or **INR 299 for ten chapters/100 pages**.
- No “unlimited” promise and no guaranteed-mark claims.

## Evidence required by Week 16

Continue only if the evidence is coherent, not because one target is met:

- approximately 25 genuine INR 99 paying families, with at least 15 beyond close friends/family, and at least ten making the higher-priced second purchase;
- at least 70% first-session activation and 50% completing three sessions in 14 days;
- at least 60% of payers use a second distinct chapter within the relevant test cycle;
- at least 50% return in a later assessment cycle within the study window;
- reviewed question defects below 2% and overturned subjective grades below 5%;
- median adult QC at or below five minutes per chapter and support below 20 minutes/family/month;
- cash CAC at or below roughly INR 700 in one repeatable channel, with at least 30% referral/community acquisition; and
- counsel confirms a workable paid-beta approach for uploads, derived banks, retention/deletion, child consent, processors, and rights-holder complaints.

Twenty-five payments from personal obligation, without child use and second-chapter retention, do not count.

## Kill conditions

Stop or materially pivot if fewer than 10 of 40 activated free-chapter families pay; fewer than 30% of payers use a second chapter; activation remains below 40%; grading overturns remain above 10%; adult QC remains above ten minutes/chapter; support remains above 45 minutes/family/month; cash CAC remains above INR 1,500 with weak referrals; or counsel identifies a rights/privacy requirement that is unavailable within the budget.

## Capital and route recommendation

Stay bootstrap-first. D2C is the fastest learning route because the parent is the proposed buyer and the founder has parent-network access. Tutor-led distribution may be tested after D2C repeat use; school sales and publisher licensing have longer cycles and should not determine the sabbatical.

Do not raise capital to compensate for weak retention or manual loss-making operations. Preserve the option to raise only after repeated paid cohorts, strong exam-cycle retention, reliable grading, and evidence that capital accelerates a working acquisition or publisher-distribution engine.

## What would reverse this recommendation

- **Upgrade to pursue more aggressively:** two consecutive cohorts meet payment, second-chapter, next-cycle, quality, cost, and referral thresholds; the founder can operate within 10-15 hours/week; and a scalable reviewer/model-routing process is demonstrated. Expand only one dimension at a time—CBSE, Grades 8-10, or another modality.
- **Downgrade to stop:** families praise the idea but do not upload/pay/return; children require as much parent prompting as the old workflow; reliable subjective grading requires uneconomic review; or a commercially workable rights/privacy posture cannot be established.

**Bottom line:** this is a more credible low-cost product wedge than initially assessed, particularly for Grades 3-7. Technical feasibility is becoming less concerning; external payment, repeat use, support and acquisition remain unproven. The recommendation remains pursue with a narrow, gated validation—not broad build-out.
