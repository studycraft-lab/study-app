## Scope and accuracy decisions

The synopsis groups vacuum tubes, transistors, integrated circuits, microprocessors, and AI into five generations. The lesson presents these as approximate classroom labels, because dates and technologies overlap. It avoids absolute “first computer” and “first programmer” claims, and does not suggest that AI replaced microprocessors. The 2024 paper includes third-generation IC and microcomputer questions; the 2023 paper includes fourth/fifth-generation, Babbage, and third-generation questions.

The first IBM System/360 models used IBM Solid Logic Technology. They are placed in the third-generation era without claiming that every original System/360 machine contained monolithic ICs.
