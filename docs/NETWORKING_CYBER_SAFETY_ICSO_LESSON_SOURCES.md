# Grade 6 ICSO: Networking and Cyber Safety

The interactive lesson is at `/study/icso/networking-cyber-safety`. It has six short missions (23 estimated minutes) and six original final questions (about 6 minutes). Its diagrams and inbox are local simulations; no real network, message or malware is opened.
The result stays on the lesson page and is not yet saved to the StudyCraft progress dashboard.

## Sources and scope

- Saved SOF synopsis: `ingestion-artifacts/icso-grade6-synopses/08-networking-and-cyber-safety.html`. The lesson covers networking and sharing, LAN/MAN/WAN, bus/ring/star topology, internet/intranet, browser/search engine, virus/worm/Trojan, and practical online safety.
- The synopsis contains two repeated copies of the same chapter text. Its browser and search-engine brand lists are examples; the lesson teaches roles rather than market rankings or product features that may change.
- Recent downloaded papers, newest first: `ICSO - 2025_Class_6_2296_answer.pdf` uses a password-risk scenario and a search-engine crawler statement item; `ICSO - 2024_Class_6_7530_answer.pdf` asks about Wi-Fi and a network-adapter image; `ICSO - 2023_Class_6_8757_answer.pdf` uses hyperlink and wireless-network identification. The app questions are newly written and focus on transferable concepts rather than image recognition.
- The downloaded mock series adds browser identification, LAN, antivirus and search-engine items. Some ask product trivia or search-engine internals beyond this synopsis, so the compact lesson prioritises the saved chapter and newer papers.
- The signed-in SOF chapter question bank was not available for this build. Review it before claiming the final challenge matches its full difficulty and coverage.

## Accuracy audit

| Concept | Cross-check |
| --- | --- |
| LAN covers a limited local area; WAN spans larger areas | [Cloudflare LAN](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/network-layer/what-is-a-lan/), [Cloudflare WAN](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/network-layer/what-is-a-wan/) |
| Star, bus and ring describe connection layouts | [Cisco network topology](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/topics/networking/what-is-network-topology.html) |
| A browser displays pages; a search engine helps find them | [MDN browsing the web](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Getting_started/Environment_setup/Browsing_the_web) |
| Virus, worm and Trojan are different malware patterns | [CISA malware tip card](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/Malware_1.pdf) |
| Pause at suspicious links, use strong passwords, update software and seek help | [CISA Secure Our World](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/secure-our-world-cybersecurity-awareness-month-resources) |

The synopsis says ring traffic moves in one direction. That is not universal, so the lesson teaches the loop shape and explicitly says traffic direction depends on the design. The 30-minute lesson is a core introduction; it does not teach every browser brand or networking device in the synopsis and papers.
