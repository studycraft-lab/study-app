# The Early Vedic Civilization — revision video

One Class 6 History chapter overview based on the five user-supplied textbook screenshots, pages 45–53. The video is 5:53, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, H.264 with AAC narration. Captions are visible in the video; separate SRT and WebVTT files are provided. The illustrations are original vector diagrams, and the map is deliberately marked as schematic.

The eight chapter markers are in `chapters.txt`. Three sections pause for the learner to answer aloud. The video explains the source, timeline, settlement, organisation, daily life, work and beliefs, then asks the learner to rebuild the chapter from memory. It is an overview for revision; the textbook exercises remain useful for practising precise names and short answers.

The script identifies the textbook's dates and broad social descriptions as its account. It does not present all families' experiences as identical. The source manifest and editable production code are in `proposals/early-vedic-video/`.
