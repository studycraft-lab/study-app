**Narration:** Offline Kokoro voice; no paid voice or video API used

Watch `wild-duck.mp4` after reading the chapter. It retells the complete plot, explains the story's key words, and asks ten spoken revision questions. Each question has a seven-second silent thinking pause followed by an answer. Pause the player for longer whenever the child needs it. The video cannot hear or evaluate spoken answers.

The narration and questions were grounded in the supplied chapter scan. The separate textbook grammar exercise on transitive and intransitive verbs is outside this literature revision. This lesson does not depend on the chapter's unfinished question bank.
